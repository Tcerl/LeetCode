1. **Verify the account:** Ask the customer for their full name, account number, and billing address. Compare this information with the records in our database to ensure security.
2. **Review the bill:** Ask the customer to identify the specific charge they believe is incorrect. Open the invoice in the billing system and check the transaction details.
3. **Determine the cause:** Check if the incorrect charge was due to a system error (such as double-billing) or a misunderstanding of fees.
4. **Take action:** 
   - If the charge is an error, process a refund immediately. Inform the customer that the refund will appear on their credit card statement within 3 to 5 business days.
   - If the charge is correct, explain the fee politely and clearly. Offer to send a detailed breakdown of fees by email.
5. **Log the call:** Enter a summary of the conversation in the customer's account notes, including the date, time, and resolution.
