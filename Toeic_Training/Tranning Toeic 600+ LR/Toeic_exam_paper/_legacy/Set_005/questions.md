**Customer Service Policy: Processing Product Exchanges**

At Fashion World, we want our customers to be happy with their purchases. If a customer wishes to exchange a clothing item for a different size or color, please follow these steps:

1. **Verify the purchase:** Ask the customer for the original store receipt. Verify that the purchase was made within the last 30 days. Under no circumstances should exchanges be processed for items bought more than 30 days ago.
2. **Inspect the item:** Ensure the clothing is unworn, unwashed, and has all original price tags still attached. If the item is damaged or displays signs of wear, politely decline the exchange.
3. **Check store stock:** Ask the customer what size or color they would like. Check our computer inventory system to see if the item is in stock.
   - If the item is in stock, retrieve it from the shelves and complete the exchange at the register.
   - If the item is out of stock, check if it is available on our website. Offer to order the item online with free home delivery.
4. **Complete the transaction:** Scan both the returned item and the new item. If there is a price difference (such as during a sale), refund the difference to the original credit card or charge the additional amount.
5. **Log the exchange:** File the receipt copy in the daily transaction folder.
