# Part 2 Drill — 01 · WH-Questions (When / Where / Who / Why / What / Which / How)

> **20 câu** · 3 lựa chọn (A/B/C) · chọn phản hồi **hợp lý nhất**
> **Cách làm text (không audio):** đọc **từ để hỏi đầu câu** → loại đáp án trả lời sai loại câu hỏi (When mà đáp "ở tầng 3" → loại).
> **Khi luyện bằng audio** (`Brain/audio_playlist.md`): nghe **3 chữ đầu**, cảnh giác 2 bẫy:
> - **Lặp âm / lặp từ**: câu hỏi có "price" → đáp án có "prices" thường là bẫy
> - **Trả lời gián tiếp**: "Where's the report?" → "Ask Minh" / "I haven't finished it" vẫn đúng
>
> Đáp án ở cuối file.

## Câu hỏi

**1.** Where can I find the updated price list?
(A) The prices went up last month.
(B) I found it very useful.
(C) It's on the shared drive under "Sales."

**2.** Who's giving the presentation at the client meeting?
(A) Diana from the product team.
(B) In the third-floor conference room.
(C) About the new pricing model.

**3.** When does the shipment need to leave the warehouse?
(A) From the loading dock.
(B) By six tomorrow morning.
(C) A shipment of office chairs.

**4.** Why was the afternoon session canceled?
(A) Yes, it was rescheduled.
(B) In about two hours.
(C) The speaker's flight was delayed.

**5.** How long will the software upgrade take?
(A) About two hours.
(B) The new version is much faster.
(C) IT is handling the upgrade.

**6.** Which supplier did we use for the last order?
(A) We ordered 500 units.
(B) I think it was Kimura Trading.
(C) The order arrived on time.

**7.** What time should I arrive for the orientation?
(A) At the main reception desk.
(B) Please bring a photo ID.
(C) Around 8:45 would be ideal.

**8.** Where are the training manuals kept?
(A) In the cabinet next to the printer.
(B) The training starts on Monday.
(C) Ms. Alvarez wrote them.

**9.** Who approved this budget increase?
(A) It went up by ten percent.
(B) The regional director did.
(C) Last Thursday, I believe.

**10.** Why don't we move the team meeting to Thursday?
(A) In meeting room B.
(B) The meeting lasted an hour.
(C) That works better for me too.

**11.** How did the product demo go?
(A) It went really well, actually.
(B) The demo is scheduled for 3 P.M.
(C) About twenty people attended.

**12.** When will the renovated lobby reopen?
(A) The renovation cost quite a lot.
(B) Not until early next month.
(C) On the ground floor.

**13.** What's causing the delay in the report?
(A) We're still waiting on data from finance.
(B) The report is about forty pages.
(C) It was delayed last quarter too.

**14.** Who should I contact about a parking permit?
(A) In the basement garage.
(B) It expires at the end of the year.
(C) Try the facilities office.

**15.** How often are the fire drills held?
(A) On the emergency staircase.
(B) Twice a year, usually.
(C) The drill went smoothly.

**16.** Where did you put the signed contracts?
(A) I left them on your desk.
(B) They were signed yesterday.
(C) With the legal department's approval.

**17.** Why is the quarterly meeting being held online this time?
(A) It's usually held in person.
(B) Several managers are traveling this week.
(C) Every three months.

**18.** What kind of feedback did the customer survey collect?
(A) About 300 people responded.
(B) The survey closed on Friday.
(C) Mostly comments about delivery times.

**19.** Which room has the video conferencing equipment?
(A) The large one at the end of the hall.
(B) The equipment was just serviced.
(C) A video call with the Tokyo office.

**20.** When are the annual performance reviews scheduled?
(A) With your direct manager.
(B) They'll take place in mid-March.
(C) The reviews were very positive.
