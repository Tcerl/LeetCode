## 5 câu điền khuyết (ôn nhanh)

1. The board could not vote because it failed to reach a ________.
2. Please ________ receipt of this email so we know it arrived.
3. I'll ________ the minutes to everyone within an hour.
4. The client meeting has been ________ to next Tuesday.
5. Keep the summary ________ — one paragraph is enough.

**KEY:** 1 quorum · 2 acknowledge · 3 circulate · 4 postponed/rescheduled · 5 concise
