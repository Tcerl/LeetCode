STATE TO REQUIRE PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARIES FOR CONSUMER CONTRACTS

Beginning next year, companies offering consumer contracts longer than ten pages — gym memberships, phone plans, and similar — must include a one-page plain-language summary of the key terms: price, length, how to cancel, and any automatic renewal.

The summary does not replace the full contract, and the full terms still control if there is a conflict. But regulators say the summary must be accurate; a misleading summary can itself be the basis of a complaint.

Businesses have a nine-month window to update their documents. The state has published a free template. Industry groups asked for an eighteen-month window and were turned down.
