### Passage 8 — Community solar FAQ (167–169)

**COMMUNITY SOLAR — FAQ**

**What is community solar?**
You subscribe to a share of a nearby solar farm. The electricity it produces is credited to your utility bill, lowering it. You don't install anything.

**How much do I save?**
Subscribers typically save 5–10% on the portion of their bill covered by the credits, after the subscription fee.

**What if I move?**
If you move within the same utility area, your subscription moves with you. If you move outside it, you can cancel with 90 days' notice, no penalty.

**Is there a contract?**
Month-to-month after the first year. The first-year commitment lets the project plan its output.

### Passages 18–20 — Air-quality guidance, email & reply (191–195)

**Document 1 — Guidance**

**OUTDOOR WORK DURING POOR AIR QUALITY — GUIDANCE**

We use the local Air Quality Index (AQI). Check it at the start of each shift and again at midday.

- AQI 0–100 (Good/Moderate): normal outdoor work.
- AQI 101–150 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups): normal work, but staff with asthma or heart conditions should limit strenuous tasks and may request lighter duties, no questions asked.
- AQI 151–200 (Unhealthy): reduce heavy exertion for everyone; take a 10-minute indoor break each hour; N95 masks available and recommended for outdoor tasks.
- AQI over 200 (Very Unhealthy+): outdoor work stops except brief essential tasks; supervisor decides.

Report worsening symptoms (cough, chest tightness, headache) to your supervisor immediately.
