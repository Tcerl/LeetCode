**HANDLING WRITTEN COMPLAINTS — STORE PROCEDURE**

1. Acknowledge every written complaint within 1 business day, even if you don't have an answer yet.
2. Investigate: check receipts, camera footage if relevant, and talk to the staff involved.
3. Reply with a decision within 5 business days. If it will take longer, tell the customer why and give a new date.
