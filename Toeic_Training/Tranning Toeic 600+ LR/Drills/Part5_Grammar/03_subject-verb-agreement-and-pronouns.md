# Part 5 Drill — 03 · Subject–Verb Agreement & Pronouns (hòa hợp chủ–vị & đại từ)

> **25 câu** · Mục tiêu ≤ 20 giây/câu
> **Luật lõi:**
> - Bỏ qua cụm chen giữa (`of..., along with..., as well as...`) → tìm **chủ ngữ chính**
> - `each / every / everyone / anyone / nobody / the number of` → **số ít**
> - `both / a number of / few / many` → **số nhiều**
> - `neither A nor B` / `either A or B` → chia theo **danh từ gần động từ nhất**
> - `information, equipment, furniture, merchandise, news` → không đếm được → số ít
> - Đại từ phải khớp **danh từ nó thay** (số & vai chủ ngữ / tân ngữ)
>
> Đáp án ở cuối file.

---

## Câu hỏi

**1.** The list of approved vendors ------- posted on the company intranet.
(A) are  (B) were  (C) is  (D) have

**2.** Each of the five departments ------- its own operating budget.
(A) has  (B) have  (C) having  (D) are having

**3.** The hiring committee ------- to announce its final decision on Thursday.
(A) plan  (B) are planning  (C) have planned  (D) plans

**4.** Neither the CEO nor the board members ------- available for comment yesterday.
(A) was  (B) were  (C) is  (D) has been

**5.** There ------- several unresolved issues with the current contract.
(A) is  (B) was  (C) has been  (D) are

**6.** All employees are responsible for keeping ------- workspaces clean and organized.
(A) his  (B) our  (C) their  (D) its

**7.** The manufacturer offers ------- clients a two-year satisfaction guarantee.
(A) their  (B) its  (C) it's  (D) theirs

**8.** Ms. Park and ------- will represent the firm at next month's trade show.
(A) I  (B) me  (C) my  (D) mine

**9.** The service award was presented to Mr. Diaz and ------- at the ceremony.
(A) she  (B) hers  (C) her  (D) herself

**10.** Roughly twenty percent of the annual budget ------- allocated to research and development.
(A) are  (B) were  (C) have been  (D) is

**11.** About thirty percent of the warehouse employees ------- enrolled in the new training program.
(A) is  (B) has  (C) are  (D) was

**12.** The regional manager, along with her three assistants, ------- attending the conference in Osaka.
(A) are  (B) is  (C) were  (D) have been

**13.** Both of the submitted proposals ------- merit, but the budget covers only one.
(A) has  (B) has had  (C) is having  (D) have

**14.** The information that you requested ------- attached to this email as a PDF.
(A) is  (B) are  (C) have  (D) were

**15.** Everyone on the project team ------- already received the revised schedule.
(A) have  (B) has  (C) are  (D) were

**16.** The new interns completed the entire inventory count by -------.
(A) theirself  (B) themself  (C) themselves  (D) their

**17.** The number of customer complaints ------- decreased sharply since the redesign.
(A) have  (B) are  (C) were  (D) has

**18.** A number of long-time clients ------- requested an extension of the trial period.
(A) has  (B) is  (C) have  (D) was

**19.** Whoever ------- the last to leave the office is responsible for locking the doors.
(A) is  (B) are  (C) were  (D) have been

**20.** The executive board reviewed the merger proposal and gave ------- full approval.
(A) their  (B) its  (C) it's  (D) theirs

**21.** Some of the merchandise ------- damaged while it was being unloaded from the truck.
(A) were  (B) have been  (C) are  (D) was

**22.** My colleague and ------- share a small office on the fifth floor.
(A) me  (B) myself  (C) I  (D) mine

**23.** The lab equipment on the third floor ------- serviced twice a year.
(A) is  (B) are  (C) have been  (D) were

**24.** If any employee has a scheduling conflict, ------- should notify a supervisor by Friday.
(A) it  (B) they  (C) one's  (D) themselves

**25.** The quality of the raw materials ------- the single biggest factor in the final product.
(A) are  (B) remain  (C) were  (D) is

## 🗝️ Đáp án & giải thích

| # | ĐA | Luật (≤14 từ) | Bẫy hay chọn nhầm |
|---|----|----|-----|
| 1 | **C** | chủ ngữ chính `list` (số ít); `of ... vendors` chỉ bổ nghĩa | hút theo `vendors` số nhiều |
| 2 | **A** | `each of ...` → số ít | hút theo `departments` |
| 3 | **D** | `committee` như một khối → số ít (và `its`) | chọn số nhiều khi nhấn mạnh từng thành viên — không phải ở đây |
| 4 | **B** | `neither A nor B` chia theo B = `members` → số nhiều | hút theo `CEO` |
| 5 | **D** | `there + [danh từ số nhiều: issues]` | quen tai "there is" |
| 6 | **C** | `all employees` (số nhiều) → `their` | `his` sai số |
| 7 | **B** | `The manufacturer` (một công ty) → `its` | `it's` = "it is" |
| 8 | **A** | chủ ngữ kép → cần đại từ chủ ngữ `I` | `me` là tân ngữ |
| 9 | **C** | sau giới từ `to` → đại từ tân ngữ `her` | `herself` cần chủ ngữ là "she" |
| 10 | **D** | `percent of + [budget: không đếm được]` → số ít | hút theo "percent" |
| 11 | **C** | `percent of + [employees: số nhiều]` → số nhiều | so với câu 10 — phải nhìn danh từ sau "of" |
| 12 | **B** | `along with ...` **không** đổi chủ ngữ → `manager` số ít | hút theo `assistants` |
| 13 | **D** | `both of ...` → số nhiều | `has` sai số |
| 14 | **A** | `information` không đếm được → số ít (mệnh đề `that...` chen giữa) | hút theo `you` |
| 15 | **B** | `everyone` → số ít | `have` sai |
| 16 | **C** | phản thân số nhiều đúng chính tả = `themselves` | "theirself / themself" không chuẩn văn viết |
| 17 | **D** | `the number of ...` → số ít | hút theo `complaints` |
| 18 | **C** | `a number of ...` → số nhiều (khác hẳn "the number of") | nhầm với câu 17 |
| 19 | **A** | `whoever` làm chủ ngữ → số ít | cảm giác "nhiều người" |
| 20 | **B** | `board` (một hội đồng) → `its` | `their` khi coi là các cá nhân — TOEIC ưu tiên `its` |
| 21 | **D** | `some of + [merchandise: không đếm được]` → số ít | cảm giác nhiều món hàng |
| 22 | **C** | chủ ngữ kép → `I` | `myself` không làm chủ ngữ độc lập |
| 23 | **A** | `equipment` không đếm được → số ít (`on the third floor` chen giữa) | hút theo `floor` / cảm giác nhiều máy |
| 24 | **B** | `any employee` số ít → dùng `they` (singular they, TOEIC chấp nhận) | `themselves` sai vai (cần làm chủ ngữ, không phản thân) |
| 25 | **D** | chủ ngữ chính `quality` số ít; `of the raw materials` bổ nghĩa | hút theo `materials` |

**Phân bố đáp án:** A×5 · B×6 · C×7 · D×7
