From: research@lungcenter.example To: Aria Bello Subject: RE: Study BR-114 — am I eligible?

Hi Aria,

  1. The exclusion is for hospitalization in the last 3 months. An ER visit 5 months ago that didn't lead to admission doesn't automatically rule you out — the study doctor will review the details at a screening visit.
  2. Travel reimbursement covers your actual costs to get to visits, including public transit fares (keep receipts), not just mileage.
  3. You wouldn't know during the study — it's a blinded study, so the group assignment stays hidden from you and the study doctor until the study ends. You'd learn afterward.

Would you like to book a screening visit? There's no obligation to continue after it.
