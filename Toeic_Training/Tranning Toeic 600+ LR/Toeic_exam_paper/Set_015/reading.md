### Passage 5 — Email (158–160)

**To:** all-staff **From:** Kitchen Manager **Subject:** Cooling Cooked Food — Do It Right

A reminder on cooling, since it came up in the practice inspection.

Cooked food that isn't served right away has to go from 135°F down to 70°F within two hours, and down to 41°F within a further four hours — six hours total. If it's not at 70 by hour two, it gets reheated to 165 and re-cooled, or tossed.

Don't put a deep, covered container of hot food straight in the walk-in. Use shallow pans, leave them uncovered until cold, and use an ice bath for dense items like rice and beans. Log the time in and the temperature at hour two.
