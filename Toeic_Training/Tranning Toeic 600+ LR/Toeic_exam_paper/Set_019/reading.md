### Passages 18–20 — Property-tax exemption rules, email & reply (191–195)

**Document 1 — Rules**

**HOMESTEAD & SENIOR PROPERTY-TAX EXEMPTIONS**

- **Homestead exemption:** Available on your primary residence only. Reduces the taxable value by $25,000. Apply once; it renews automatically as long as you own and live in the home.
- **Senior exemption:** For owners 65+ with household income under the annual limit. Reduces taxable value by an additional $30,000. Must be renewed every year with an income statement.
- **Deadline:** Applications are due March 1 for that tax year. Applications received after March 1 apply to the next year.
- **Change of use:** If the home stops being your primary residence (you rent it out, or move), you must notify the assessor within 60 days. Exemptions claimed after you no longer qualify are billed back with interest.
- Both exemptions can be held at the same time if you qualify for each.
