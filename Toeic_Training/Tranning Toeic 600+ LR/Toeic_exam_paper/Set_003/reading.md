**Document 1 — Terms**

**OVERDRAFT PROTECTION — How It Works**

You can link your checking account to either a savings account or a personal line of credit for overdraft protection.

- **Linked to savings:** If a payment would overdraw your checking account, we move funds from savings to cover it. Transfer fee: $10 per day on which a transfer occurs (no fee if the shortfall is under $5).
- **Linked to a line of credit:** We advance funds from the line of credit. No transfer fee, but interest applies on the advanced amount at your line's rate.
- Without any protection, a rejected payment incurs a $30 returned-item fee.
