MID-YEAR CHECK-IN — How It Works

The mid-year check-in is a lighter version of the annual review. It is not scored and does not affect pay.

  1. Employee fills in a short self-reflection (3 questions) in the HR system by June 15.
  2. Manager reviews it and adds brief comments by June 22.
  3. Employee and manager meet for 30–45 minutes by June 30 to discuss progress and adjust goals if needed.
  4. Manager marks the check-in complete. No sign-off from the employee is required.

The purpose is to catch issues early, not to grade. The scored review happens in December.
