**Company Policy: Budget Approvals for Marketing Campaigns**

To ensure control of company expenses, all departments must follow these guidelines when planning marketing budgets:

1. **Draft the proposal:** The project leader must write a detailed proposal outlining the campaign goals, targeted channels (e.g. print, social media), and estimated costs.
2. **Review limits:** The proposed budget must fit within the department's yearly limits.
   - For campaigns costing **under $1,000**, approval from the department head is sufficient.
   - For campaigns costing **between $1,000 and $5,000**, approval from the marketing director is required.
   - For campaigns costing **over $5,000**, the proposal must be submitted to the finance committee.
3. **Submit invoice:** Once the proposal is approved, the project leader can place orders with external suppliers. All supplier invoices must be forwarded to the accounting office within 5 business days.
4. **Log expenses:** Enter the final costs in the company expense database, including the date, project ID, and resolution.
