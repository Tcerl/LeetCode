# Part 5 Drill — 06 · Collocations (từ đi với từ)

> **25 câu** · Mục tiêu ≤ 20 giây/câu
> **Luật lõi:** 4 lựa chọn gần nghĩa nhau — chỉ **một từ** đi đúng với danh từ/ngữ cảnh đó.
> Đây là câu **từ vựng**, không phải ngữ pháp: học thuộc cụm, không suy luận.
> Đáp án ở cuối file. Cụm cần thuộc nằm ở bảng cuối.

---

## Câu hỏi

**1.** The research team will ------- a survey to measure customer satisfaction this quarter.
(A) make  (B) conduct  (C) do  (D) build

**2.** Factories in the region are struggling to ------- the growing demand for electric vehicles.
(A) reach  (B) receive  (C) collect  (D) meet

**3.** Customers who wish to ------- an order may call the hotline or use the website.
(A) do  (B) make  (C) place  (D) hold

**4.** After a long discussion, the committee finally ------- a decision on the vendor.
(A) reached  (B) arrived  (C) achieved  (D) gained

**5.** Several team members have ------- concerns about the new scheduling system.
(A) lifted  (B) raised  (C) grown  (D) built

**6.** The client has asked us to ------- the submission deadline by one week.
(A) prolong  (B) enlarge  (C) widen  (D) extend

**7.** Please ------- your completed expense report to the finance department by Friday.
(A) deliver  (B) pass  (C) submit  (D) send in

**8.** The manager called a meeting to ------- the recurring quality problem on the assembly line.
(A) address  (B) speak  (C) reply  (D) respond

**9.** As a one-time courtesy, the airline agreed to ------- the ticket change fee.
(A) wave  (B) waive  (C) refuse  (D) deny

**10.** The store will ------- a full refund if the item is returned within 30 days.
(A) give back  (B) return  (C) issue  (D) pay off

**11.** Our lower pricing gives the company a competitive ------- over other firms in the sector.
(A) corner  (B) angle  (C) point  (D) edge

**12.** Tenants must give 60 days' ------- notice before terminating the lease.
(A) prior  (B) early  (C) fore  (D) former

**13.** The travel itinerary is still ------- and may change depending on the weather.
(A) temporary  (B) tentative  (C) doubtful  (D) unstable

**14.** The updated data-privacy regulation will ------- into effect on January 1.
(A) go  (B) get  (C) come  (D) turn

**15.** All on-site contractors are required to ------- the building's safety guidelines at all times.
(A) watch  (B) look at  (C) notice  (D) observe

**16.** The two software companies have decided to ------- forces to build the new platform.
(A) join  (B) connect  (C) unite  (D) attach

**17.** The new sales director quickly ------- a strong impression on the leadership team.
(A) did  (B) made  (C) took  (D) gave

**18.** At the next board meeting, the finance director will ------- the annual budget.
(A) show  (B) display  (C) present  (D) exhibit

**19.** The company must ------- action immediately to prevent further financial losses.
(A) make  (B) do  (C) have  (D) take

**20.** The holiday marketing campaign is expected to ------- sales significantly in December.
(A) boost  (B) rise  (C) grow  (D) climb

**21.** The company hopes to ------- a larger share of the Southeast Asian market by 2028.
(A) catch  (B) capture  (C) grab  (D) hold on

**22.** Attendance at the annual compliance training is ------- for all new employees.
(A) forced  (B) demanded  (C) compulsory  (D) obliged

**23.** The airline offers ------- rates to customers who book at least 21 days in advance.
(A) lowered  (B) cheapened  (C) cut  (D) discounted

**24.** The manager asked the support team to ------- priority to the most urgent tickets.
(A) give  (B) make  (C) put  (D) set

**25.** The internal audit report ------- light on why the project fell behind schedule.
(A) throws  (B) sheds  (C) brings  (D) makes

## 🗝️ Đáp án & giải thích

| # | ĐA | Cụm đúng | Vì sao loại từ khác |
|---|----|----|-----|
| 1 | **B** | `conduct a survey / research` | make/do/build không đi với "survey" trong văn TOEIC |
| 2 | **D** | `meet demand` (đáp ứng nhu cầu) | reach/receive/collect + demand đều sai |
| 3 | **C** | `place an order` | make/do/hold + an order sai |
| 4 | **A** | `reach a decision` | arrive **at** a decision (thiếu "at"); achieve/gain sai |
| 5 | **B** | `raise concerns` | lift/grow/build + concerns sai |
| 6 | **D** | `extend a deadline` | prolong/enlarge/widen không đi với "deadline" |
| 7 | **C** | `submit a report` | send **in** (thiếu "in"); deliver/pass sai đăng ký từ |
| 8 | **A** | `address a problem / an issue` (giải quyết) | speak/reply/respond cần giới từ (to) |
| 9 | **B** | `waive a fee` (miễn phí) | `wave` = vẫy tay (bẫy đồng âm); refuse/deny sai nghĩa |
| 10 | **C** | `issue a refund` | give back/return/pay off + a refund sai |
| 11 | **D** | `competitive edge` (lợi thế cạnh tranh) | corner/angle/point sai cụm |
| 12 | **A** | `prior notice` (báo trước) | early/fore/former sai cụm |
| 13 | **B** | `tentative` (tạm thời, chưa chốt — cho lịch/kế hoạch) | temporary = tạm (về thời hạn); doubtful/unstable sai sắc thái |
| 14 | **C** | `come into effect` (có hiệu lực) | go/get/turn + into effect sai |
| 15 | **D** | `observe guidelines / rules` (tuân thủ) | watch/look at/notice = nhìn thấy, không phải tuân theo |
| 16 | **A** | `join forces` (hợp lực) | connect/unite/attach + forces sai cụm |
| 17 | **B** | `make an impression` | do/take/give + an impression sai |
| 18 | **C** | `present a budget / plan / report` | show/display/exhibit dùng cho vật thể trưng bày |
| 19 | **D** | `take action` | make/do/have + action sai |
| 20 | **A** | `boost sales` (đẩy mạnh) | rise/climb là nội động từ; grow sales ít trang trọng hơn |
| 21 | **B** | `capture market share` | catch/grab/hold on sai đăng ký từ |
| 22 | **C** | `compulsory` = bắt buộc (bổ ngữ cho "is") | forced/demanded/obliged cần cấu trúc khác |
| 23 | **D** | `discounted rates / prices` | lowered/cheapened/cut không đi trực tiếp trước "rates" ở đây |
| 24 | **A** | `give priority to` | make/put/set + priority to sai |
| 25 | **B** | `shed light on` (làm sáng tỏ) | throw/bring/make + light on sai cụm |

**Phân bố đáp án:** A×6 · B×7 · C×6 · D×6

## 📚 Collocation TOEIC tần suất cao — thuộc cả cụm

| Động từ + danh từ | Động từ + danh từ |
|---|---|
| conduct a survey / research / an interview | meet a deadline / demand / requirements / expectations |
| place / cancel / process an order | reach a decision / an agreement / a compromise |
| raise concerns / awareness / prices / funds | address / resolve / tackle an issue |
| extend a deadline / an offer / an invitation / a contract | submit an application / a proposal / a report |
| issue a refund / a statement / a permit / an apology | take action / measures / responsibility / effect |
| waive a fee / a requirement / the right | boost / increase / drive sales |
| make a decision / a profit / an impression / progress | give / grant approval; give priority; give notice |
| launch / release / recall a product | come into effect / into force / to an agreement |

| Tính từ + danh từ |
|---|
| competitive edge / advantage / price · prior notice / experience / approval · tentative schedule / agreement · outstanding balance / performance · strict guidelines / deadline · valued customer · proven track record · full refund |
