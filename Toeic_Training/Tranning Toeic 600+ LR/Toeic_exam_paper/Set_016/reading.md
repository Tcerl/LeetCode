**Document 1 — Reference**

**PROJECT DELIVERY METHODS**

| Method | How it works | Owner's cost certainty | Owner's involvement | Best when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design-Bid-Build | Design fully, then bid to contractors, then build | High at bid, low during design | High | Scope is well defined; low change risk |

| Construction Manager at Risk | CM joins during design, gives a guaranteed maximum price | Medium-high once GMP is set | Medium | Complex project; owner wants builder input during design |

Design-Bid-Build usually takes the longest overall because bidding can't start until design is complete.

Hi Ellis,

With a 14-month deadline and an evolving program, Design-Bid-Build is the wrong fit — you'd lose months waiting for a complete design before bidding, and mid-design changes would be painful.
