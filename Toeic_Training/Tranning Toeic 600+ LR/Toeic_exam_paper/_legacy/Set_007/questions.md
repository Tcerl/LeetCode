**Customer Service Policy Guide — Handling Damaged Cargo Claims**

When a customer contacts support regarding cargo damaged during shipping, representatives must follow these steps to process the claim:

1. **Verify the account:** Ask the customer for their full name, shipping account number, and tracking number. Compare this information with the records in our database to ensure security.
2. **Review the claim details:** Ask the customer to identify the specific items that were damaged. Open the delivery invoice in the shipping system and check the package details.
3. **Request proof:** Ask the customer to email photos of the damaged packaging boxes and items. Also request a copy of the delivery receipt signed by the courier driver.
4. **Take action:**
   - If the claim is verified, process a replacement shipment immediately at no extra charge. If the customer prefers a refund, inform them that the refund will appear on their credit card statement within 3 to 5 business days.
   - If the claim is incomplete, request the missing documents by email and log the status.
5. **Log the claim:** Enter a summary of the conversation in the customer's account notes, including the date, time, and resolution.
