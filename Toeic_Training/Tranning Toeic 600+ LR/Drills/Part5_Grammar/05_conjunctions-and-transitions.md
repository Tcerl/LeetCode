# Part 5 Drill — 05 · Conjunctions vs Transitions vs Prepositions

> **25 câu** · Mục tiêu ≤ 20 giây/câu
> **Luật lõi — nhìn cái gì đứng sau chỗ trống:**
> - **+ mệnh đề (có S + V)** → **liên từ**: `although, even though, while, whereas, because, since, as, unless, if, once, as soon as, so that, before, after`
> - **+ danh từ / cụm danh từ** → **giới từ**: `despite, in spite of, because of, due to, owing to, during, prior to`
> - **đứng sau dấu `;` hoặc đầu câu, có dấu phẩy** → **trạng từ liên kết**: `however, therefore, moreover, nevertheless, consequently, otherwise, meanwhile, as a result`
> - Nghĩa: tương phản (`although / however / despite`) vs nguyên nhân (`because / therefore / due to`)
>
> Đáp án ở cuối file.

---

## Câu hỏi

**1.** ------- the product received excellent reviews, sales remained disappointing.
(A) Despite  (B) Because of  (C) Although  (D) However

**2.** The launch was delayed ------- a problem with the main supplier.
(A) although  (B) because  (C) despite  (D) because of

**3.** The team met the deadline ------- several unexpected setbacks.
(A) even though  (B) despite  (C) because  (D) therefore

**4.** Ms. Lopez will lead the project ------- Mr. Chen is on parental leave.
(A) while  (B) during  (C) despite  (D) however

**5.** The main road was closed for repairs; -------, commuters had to take a detour.
(A) although  (B) because  (C) whereas  (D) as a result

**6.** ------- the budget was approved, the team began purchasing equipment.
(A) Once  (B) Despite  (C) In spite of  (D) However

**7.** The new software is powerful; -------, it takes some time to learn.
(A) therefore  (B) however  (C) moreover  (D) for example

**8.** Employees may work from home ------- they maintain their usual productivity.
(A) in spite of  (B) due to  (C) as long as  (D) despite

**9.** ------- the heavy snowfall overnight, all morning flights departed on schedule.
(A) Although  (B) Even though  (C) Because  (D) In spite of

**10.** The factory increased its output ------- reducing energy costs at the same time.
(A) while  (B) during  (C) despite  (D) however

**11.** We should order more supplies today; -------, we may run out by next week.
(A) however  (B) otherwise  (C) moreover  (D) therefore

**12.** ------- demand has risen steadily, the company plans to hire more staff.
(A) Despite  (B) During  (C) Since  (D) However

**13.** The contract was signed ------- some minor disagreements over the payment terms.
(A) although  (B) even though  (C) because  (D) despite

**14.** The presentation was very informative; -------, it ran far too long.
(A) nevertheless  (B) therefore  (C) furthermore  (D) similarly

**15.** Please review the attached file ------- you attend tomorrow's meeting.
(A) prior to  (B) before  (C) despite  (D) during

**16.** ------- the manager approved the request, the funds were transferred immediately.
(A) Despite  (B) Because of  (C) As soon as  (D) In addition

**17.** The store extended its opening hours ------- the holiday shopping season.
(A) while  (B) although  (C) because  (D) during

**18.** The proposal is over budget; -------, it fails to address the delivery timeline.
(A) however  (B) therefore  (C) moreover  (D) otherwise

**19.** ------- he had relatively little experience, Mr. Reyes was promoted to team lead.
(A) Despite  (B) Even though  (C) In spite of  (D) Because of

**20.** The company relocated its headquarters ------- the lower operating costs in the region.
(A) because  (B) so that  (C) due to  (D) although

**21.** Sales rose sharply in the first quarter; -------, they leveled off after June.
(A) moreover  (B) therefore  (C) as a result  (D) however

**22.** ------- you submit the form by 5 P.M. today, it will not be processed until Monday.
(A) Unless  (B) Despite  (C) Because of  (D) However

**23.** The equipment must be inspected regularly ------- it continues to operate safely.
(A) so that  (B) despite  (C) because of  (D) in order

**24.** ------- its higher monthly price, the premium plan offers far better technical support.
(A) Although  (B) Despite  (C) Because  (D) Since

**25.** The vendor missed the final deadline; -------, we have decided to end the contract.
(A) however  (B) nevertheless  (C) consequently  (D) meanwhile

## 📚 Xếp nhóm để nhớ

| Ý | Liên từ (+ mệnh đề) | Giới từ (+ danh từ) | Trạng từ liên kết (sau `;`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tương phản | although, even though, though, while, whereas | despite, in spite of | however, nevertheless, nonetheless, on the other hand |
| Nguyên nhân → kết quả | because, since, as | because of, due to, owing to | therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, hence |
| Bổ sung | — | in addition to, besides | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides |
| Điều kiện | if, unless, as long as, provided that | in case of | otherwise |
| Thời gian | when, while, before, after, once, as soon as, until | during, prior to, following | meanwhile, subsequently |
