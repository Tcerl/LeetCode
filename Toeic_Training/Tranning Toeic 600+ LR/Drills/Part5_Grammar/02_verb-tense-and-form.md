# Part 5 Drill — 02 · Verb Tense & Form (thì và dạng động từ)

> **25 câu** · Mục tiêu ≤ 20 giây/câu
> **Luật lõi:**
> - Tìm **mốc thời gian**: `yesterday / last week / in 2019` → quá khứ đơn · `since / for / over the past` → hiện tại hoàn thành · `next / tomorrow / by [tương lai]` → tương lai
> - Mệnh đề thời gian (`when, as soon as, once, by the time, after`) — **không dùng `will`**, dùng hiện tại thay tương lai
> - Chủ ngữ **nhận** hành động → **bị động** (`be + P.P.`)
> - `suggest / recommend / consider / avoid` + **V-ing** · `plan / decide / agree / hope` + **to V**
>
> Đáp án ở cuối file.

---

## Câu hỏi

**1.** The shipment ------- yesterday, three days ahead of schedule.
(A) arrives  (B) arrived  (C) has arrived  (D) will arrive

**2.** By the time the auditors arrive next Monday, the finance team ------- all the records.
(A) will organize  (B) has organized  (C) organized  (D) will have organized

**3.** The CEO ------- the annual shareholders' meeting every year since 2015.
(A) attends  (B) attended  (C) has attended  (D) is attending

**4.** All grant applications must ------- no later than March 31.
(A) be submitted  (B) submit  (C) submitting  (D) to submit

**5.** Once the new safety regulations ------- into effect, every employee must wear an ID badge.
(A) will come  (B) come  (C) came  (D) coming

**6.** The design team ------- on the prototype for three weeks before management finally approved it.
(A) works  (B) has worked  (C) will work  (D) had been working

**7.** Ms. Lee ------- to the Singapore office at the start of the next quarter.
(A) transfers  (B) transferring  (C) will be transferred  (D) has transferred

**8.** If the supplier ------- the delivery deadline, the factory will switch to another vendor.
(A) misses  (B) will miss  (C) missed  (D) has missed

**9.** The building's fire alarm system ------- every six months by a licensed contractor.
(A) inspects  (B) inspecting  (C) has inspecting  (D) is inspected

**10.** We regret to inform you that your reserved flight ------- canceled because of the storm.
(A) has  (B) has been  (C) having  (D) being

**11.** During the meeting, the director suggested ------- the launch date to early June.
(A) to postpone  (B) postpone  (C) postponing  (D) postponed

**12.** The company plans ------- its distribution network into three new provinces next year.
(A) to expand  (B) expanding  (C) expanded  (D) expansion

**13.** ------- the quarterly figures, the analyst noticed several data-entry errors.
(A) Reviewed  (B) To be reviewed  (C) Review  (D) Reviewing

**14.** Please do not enter the lab; the equipment ------- by the technicians right now.
(A) repairs  (B) is being repaired  (C) has repaired  (D) repaired

**15.** Regional sales figures ------- steadily over the past six months.
(A) rise  (B) rose  (C) are rising  (D) have risen

**16.** Under the contract, either party ------- the agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.
(A) terminate  (B) terminating  (C) may terminate  (D) to terminate

**17.** The assembly line ------- at full capacity until the upgraded machines were installed last month.
(A) was not operating  (B) is not operating  (C) has not operated  (D) will not operate

**18.** Warehouse staff must make sure that all fragile items ------- clearly before shipping.
(A) label  (B) labeling  (C) are labeled  (D) to label

**19.** The election committee will announce the outcome as soon as the ballots ------- counted.
(A) will be  (B) are  (C) have  (D) were

**20.** Over the last decade, the district ------- into one of the region's main financial centers.
(A) develops  (B) developed  (C) had developed  (D) has developed

**21.** All new hires ------- to complete the compliance course within their first month.
(A) expect  (B) are expected  (C) expecting  (D) has expected

**22.** As of last Friday, the construction project ------- about two weeks behind schedule.
(A) falls  (B) is falling  (C) had fallen  (D) will fall

**23.** The parent company ------- its headquarters from Chicago to Denver in 2019.
(A) relocated  (B) relocates  (C) has relocated  (D) had relocated

**24.** Under no circumstances ------- the login credentials be shared with anyone outside the team.
(A) must not  (B) not  (C) do  (D) must

**25.** The interns ------- orientation sessions all this week, so the training room is unavailable.
(A) undergo  (B) undergoes  (C) are undergoing  (D) have undergone

| 2 | **D** | `by the time + [tương lai]` → **future perfect** | tương lai đơn thiếu ý "hoàn thành trước" |

| 4 | **A** | applications **được** nộp → `must be + P.P.` | dạng chủ động sai vai |

| 6 | **D** | hành động kéo dài **trước** một mốc quá khứ → past perfect continuous | hiện tại hoàn thành sai mốc |
| 7 | **C** | tương lai + chủ ngữ **được** chuyển → `will be + P.P.` | dạng chủ động, sai thì |
| 8 | **A** | điều kiện loại 1: `if + hiện tại`, mệnh đề chính `will` | không dùng `will` sau `if` |
| 9 | **D** | system **được** kiểm tra định kỳ → bị động | dạng chủ động sai vai |
| 10 | **B** | `flight` **bị** hủy → hiện tại hoàn thành bị động `has been + P.P.` | "has" thiếu `been` |
| 11 | **C** | `suggest` + **V-ing** | "suggest to V" là lỗi kinh điển |
| 12 | **A** | `plan` + **to V** | "plan V-ing" sai |
| 13 | **D** | phân từ hiện tại (chủ ngữ **chủ động** review) | phân từ quá khứ = bị động, sai nghĩa |
| 14 | **B** | `right now` + máy **được** sửa → hiện tại tiếp diễn bị động | "has repaired" chủ động sai |
| 15 | **D** | `over the past six months` → hiện tại hoàn thành | quá khứ đơn thiếu liên hệ tới hiện tại |
| 16 | **C** | cần **modal + V nguyên** | các dạng còn lại không có trợ động từ |
| 17 | **A** | `until + [quá khứ]` → quá khứ tiếp diễn cho nền | hiện tại hoàn thành sai mốc |
| 18 | **C** | `items` **được** dán nhãn → bị động | dạng chủ động sai vai |
| 19 | **B** | mệnh đề `as soon as` chỉ tương lai → hiện tại đơn (bị động `are counted`) | `will` sai trong mệnh đề thời gian |
| 20 | **D** | `over the last decade` → hiện tại hoàn thành | quá khứ hoàn thành cần mốc quá khứ thứ 2 |
| 21 | **B** | `hires` **được** kỳ vọng → bị động `are expected to` | dạng chủ động sai vai |
| 22 | **C** | `as of last Friday` = tính đến mốc quá khứ → quá khứ hoàn thành | hiện tại đơn sai mốc |
| 23 | **A** | `in 2019` → quá khứ đơn | có mốc thời gian xác định → không dùng HT hoàn thành |
