### Passages 15–17 — Event-type reference, email & reply (186–190)

**Document 1 — Reference**

**FUNDRAISING EVENT TYPES**

| Type | Typical net margin | Staff/volunteer load | Best for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gala / dinner | 40–55% of gross | Very high | Major donors, sponsors, visibility | High fixed costs if attendance is low |
| Fun run / walk | 60–75% | High (peer fundraising) | Broad reach, new donors, younger crowd | Weather; needs many participants |
| House parties (small, hosted) | 80–90% | Low per event | Deepening mid-level donors | Small totals unless done in series |
| Online campaign (matched) | 90%+ | Medium | Existing list, urgency moments | Fatigue if overused |

Galas raise the most per event but keep the least per dollar. A series of small house parties often nets more with less risk, but takes longer.

**Document 3 — Reply**

**From:** consult@fundwise.example **To:** Owen Blake **Subject:** RE: Rethinking our annual gala

Hi Owen,

Your gala's net margin — about 46 percent — is normal for a gala, but the staff cost is the real price. Given your strong mid-level base, I'd test replacing the gala with a series of six to eight small hosted house parties over a season, each hosted by a board member.

House parties net 80–90 percent and let you actually talk to those $250–$2,000 donors, who often move up when they feel seen. The tradeoff is they raise less in total the first year and take sustained effort rather than one big push.

Keep one visibility event — maybe a low-cost annual open house — so you don't lose the public moment the gala provided.

**VOLUNTEER POLICY — KEY POINTS**

- **Minimum commitment:** Direct-service roles (tutoring, mentoring, front desk) ask for one shift a week for at least three months, so clients have consistency. One-time and event roles have no minimum.
- **Screening:** Roles working with children or vulnerable adults require a background check (about 10 business days) and two references. Other roles require only the application.
- **Orientation:** All volunteers do a 90-minute general orientation. Direct-service volunteers also do a role-specific training before their first shift.
- **Under 18:** Ages 16–17 may volunteer for event and group roles with a signed parent form; direct-service roles are 18+.
- **Hours:** Logged in the portal. We provide letters verifying hours for school or court requirements on request.
