**Customer Service Policy Guide — Handling Billing Inquiries**

When a patient contacts support regarding a medical billing error, representatives must follow these steps to resolve the issue:

1. **Verify the account:** Ask the patient for their full name, medical record number, and home address. Compare this information with the records in our database to ensure security.
2. **Review the bill:** Ask the patient to identify the specific checkup fee they believe is incorrect. Open the invoice in the billing system and check the test details.
3. **Determine the cause:** Check if the incorrect charge was due to an insurance processing error or a misunderstanding of co-pays.
4. **Take action:**
   - If the charge is an error, process a refund immediately. Inform the patient that the refund will appear on their credit card statement within 3 to 5 business days.
   - If the charge is correct, explain the fee politely and clearly. Offer to send a detailed breakdown of fees by email.
5. **Log the call:** Enter a summary of the conversation in the patient's account notes, including the date, time, and resolution.
