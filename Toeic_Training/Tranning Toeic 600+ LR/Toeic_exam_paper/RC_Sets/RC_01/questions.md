## PART 7 — Reading Comprehension (29–40)

### Passage A — Notice (29–31)

**GRANDVIEW PUBLIC LIBRARY — Temporary Schedule Change**

From July 1 to July 14, the Grandview Public Library will operate on reduced hours while we upgrade our computer systems. During this period, the library will be open Monday through Friday from 10:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M. and will be closed on weekends.

The following services will be unavailable during the upgrade:
- Online catalog searches from outside the building
- Printing and photocopying
- Meeting room reservations

Book borrowing and returns will continue as normal. Due dates for all items currently checked out have been extended to July 20, and no late fees will be charged during the upgrade period.

We apologize for any inconvenience. Normal hours (Monday–Saturday, 9:00 A.M. to 8:00 P.M.) will resume on July 15.

**29.** Why is the library changing its hours?
(A) It is upgrading its computer systems.
(B) It is being repainted.
(C) It is reducing its staff.
(D) It is relocating to a new building.

**30.** Which service remains available during the upgrade period?
(A) Photocopying
(B) Meeting room reservations
(C) Searching the catalog from home
(D) Borrowing and returning books

**31.** What will happen on July 15?
(A) Late fees will double.
(B) The library will close for two more weeks.
(C) Normal operating hours will resume.
(D) A new online catalog will launch.
