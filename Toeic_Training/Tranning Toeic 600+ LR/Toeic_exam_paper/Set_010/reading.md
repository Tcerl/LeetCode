- Requests are fulfilled within 15 business days. Urgent requests (for an upcoming appointment with another provider) are prioritized — usually within 3 days — if marked urgent.
- To send records to another provider or a third party, a signed authorization form is required, specifying what to release and to whom.
- Certain notes (e.g., psychotherapy notes) require separate specific authorization.
- Records are provided electronically by default; paper copies on request.
