**BILL ASSISTANCE & PAYMENT OPTIONS**

- **Payment arrangement:** Any customer with a past-due balance can split it over up to 6 months, added to current bills. One arrangement at a time; a broken arrangement can't be renewed for 12 months.
- **Low-income discount:** 20% off the total bill for households under the income limit. Apply once a year with proof of income or enrollment in another assistance program.
- **Medical protection:** With a doctor's form, service won't be disconnected for non-payment while someone in the home depends on electrically powered medical equipment. It does not erase the balance.
- **Emergency grant:** Up to $400 once every 24 months toward a past-due balance, for households facing disconnection. Funds are limited and first-come.
- Disconnection for non-payment does not happen on a Friday, a weekend, a holiday, or when the forecast high is below 32°F or above 95°F.

### Passages 21–23 — Rate-change notice, email & reply (196–200)

**Document 1 — Notice**

**RATE CHANGES EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1 — RESIDENTIAL**

1. **Fixed monthly charge** rises from $10 to $14. This covers metering, billing, and grid connection regardless of usage.
2. **Energy charge** drops by about 4%. For the average home, the two changes roughly cancel out; very low-usage homes will see a small increase, high-usage homes a small decrease.
3. **Time-of-use peak window** shifts from 3–8 P.M. to 4–9 P.M. to match actual system peak.
4. **Low-income discount** increases from 15% to 20% of the total bill.

A bill-comparison tool on our site shows your estimated change based on your last 12 months.
